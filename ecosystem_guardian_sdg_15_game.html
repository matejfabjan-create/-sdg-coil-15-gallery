SDG 15

UN Life on Land Field Guide

Target 15.1 & 15.2 — Sustainable Forests & Ecosystems Conserve and restore terrestrial and freshwater ecosystems. In-game, planting Native Trees (🌲) increases passive Eco-Energy generation and shelters wildlife.
Target 15.3 — Combat Desertification Restore degraded land and soil affected by drought and floods. Use Restore Wetlands (💧) to convert barren desert tiles back into fertile soil and protect neighboring tiles from wildfires.
Target 15.5 & 15.7 — Halt Biodiversity Loss & Poaching Protect threatened species and end illegal logging/poaching. Deploy Eco-Rangers (🚒) to stop fires, loggers, and invasive weeds, and build Wildlife Corridors (🦌) between forests to spawn endangered animals!
